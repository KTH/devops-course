## Description

We want to cover differents kind of fuzzing (Whitebox, Greybox, Darkbox...) and explain their actual implementations in CI pipelines leveraging real world examples.

It allows to prevent software bugs and securities issues.

**Relevance**

Fuzzing is part of the security standards promoted by ISO for environments where lives are at stakes. It can also be leveraged to detect bugs caused by unexpected inputs, not covered by other form of testing at first.

Furthermore, it's a powerfull and easy tool to integrate in your CI pipeline.
