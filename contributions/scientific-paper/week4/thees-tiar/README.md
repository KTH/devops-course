## Description

The request for Trustworthy AI has been rising constantly, especially ever since the European Union published their [Ethics Guidlines for Trustworthy AI](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/ethics-guidelines-trustworthy-ai).
In [Towards Trustworthy Machine Learning in Production](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3708497), the authors propose that making AI trustworthy isn't just about ethics. It's about making the technology itself more robust.
They introduce the "Robustness in MLOps" approach, a way to integrate features like fairness, security, and reliability directly into mlops. 

We will follow the outline presented by the paper, focusing on topics relevant to the course.

**Relevance**

With new regulations and a general public concern for AI, the need for a trustworthy, and therefore robust, AI is bigger than ever. 
This paper delves into how this can be achieved through MLOps.
