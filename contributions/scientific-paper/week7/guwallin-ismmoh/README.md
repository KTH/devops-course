## Description

We will present the research article _A Case Study of Developer Bots: Motivations, Perceptions, and Challenges_, which investigates the usage of developer bots to aid in development workflows in CI/CD. The article is a case study conducted at Microsoft where 23 developer bots were analyzed across 13000 repositories.

The authors classify the bots into several categories. Through analyzing half a million bot actions and conducting interviews and surveys with bot developers and users, they describe software workflows that motivate the usage of bots and the benefits and challenges with their usage.  

[Link to paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3611643.3616248)

**Relevance**

The paper is relevant to DevOps as it investigates the use of software bots to aid in different stages of a CI/CD pipeline, which ties into several DevOps principles such as automation.
