## Description

The paper “SoK: A Defense-Oriented Evaluation of Software Supply Chain Security” presents the AStRA model, a framework for analyzing the elements of a software supply chain and their dependencies. 
It shifts focus from describing attacks to evaluating defensive strategies, to show how they mitigate common threats. 
The paper’s goals are mainly two: the first one is to validate his model, and the second one is to identify emergent research gaps and propose opportunities to develop novel software development tools and systems that are secure-by-design.

- _Link to article_: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14993

**Relevance**

The paper is relevant to DevOps and DevSecOps as it addresses securing CI/CD pipelines and embedding security into the development lifecycle. Its focus on dependencies and defenses directly connected to dependency management aims to show how to make software delivery more secure.
